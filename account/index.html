Your calendar

Add your held dates to your phone’s calendar

Every date your robot holds shows in your own private calendar. Keep the link to yourself: anyone with it can see the holds.

iPhone

  1. Open the Calendar app. Tap Calendars at the bottom.
  2. Tap Add Calendar, then Add Subscription Calendar.
  3. Paste your link and tap Find, then Add.

Android

  1. In Chrome, open your calendar’s website and sign in (menu, Desktop site).
  2. Next to Other calendars, tap +, then From URL.
  3. Paste your link and tap Add calendar. It shows on your phone.
